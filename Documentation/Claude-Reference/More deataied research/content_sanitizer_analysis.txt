This issue extends far beyond a single product name. The audio equipment industry,

**Technical Specifications in Filenames:** - Sample rate indicators: "96kHz", "192kHz",

The QiuYannnn methodology's effectiveness depends entirely on the ability to analyze

### Strategy 1: Technical Content Whitelisting System

The most robust approach involves creating a comprehensive whitelist of legitimate
technical terms and product identifiers that should bypass content sanitization. This
strategy requires building and maintaining a database of known legitimate technical
content.

**Implementation Approach:**

The whitelisting system would operate as a preprocessing layer before content reaches
Apple's Foundation Models. When a filename or content string is identified, the system
first checks against the whitelist before applying standard sanitization rules.

**Whitelist Categories:**

_Audio Equipment Manufacturers and Products_ : A comprehensive database of legitimate
audio equipment manufacturers, product lines, and model numbers. This would include
major brands like sE Electronics, Neumann, RODE, Shure, Audio-Technica, and hundreds
of others, along with their complete product catalogs.

_Technical Specifications_ : Standard audio technical terms including sample rates, bit
depths, channel configurations, and recording techniques that are commonly found in
professional audio filenames.

_Industry Standard Terminology_ : Professional audio terminology that might trigger false
positives, including recording techniques, studio equipment designations, and technical
measurement units.

**Maintenance and Updates:**

The whitelist system requires ongoing maintenance to remain effective. New products
are constantly released in the audio equipment industry, and the database must be


updated regularly to include new model numbers and product lines. This could be
implemented through:

```
Automated updates from manufacturer databases
Community contributions from audio professionals
Machine learning systems that identify new legitimate technical terms
Integration with professional audio equipment databases
```
**Advantages of Whitelisting:**

This approach provides the highest level of accuracy for known technical content. Once
a term is whitelisted, it can be processed without interference, ensuring consistent
results. The system can be made highly specific to the audio equipment domain,
reducing false positives while maintaining safety for other content types.

**Challenges and Limitations:**

The primary challenge is the maintenance burden. Audio equipment manufacturers
release new products regularly, and keeping the whitelist current requires ongoing
effort. Additionally, the system must handle variations in naming conventions,
abbreviations, and technical specifications that may not exactly match whitelist entries.

_Filename Structure Patterns_ : Professional audio files often follow specific naming

_Directory Context_ : Files located in directories with audio-related names or containing

_Metadata Correlation_ : Audio file metadata can provide additional context to validate that
